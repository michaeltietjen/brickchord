Your own drum kits for Bricklab

Make a folder here for each kit, and put WAV one-shots in it:

  samples/
    My Kit/
      kick.wav  snare.wav  clap.wav  rim.wav  tom.wav  hat.wav  ohat.wav  shaker.wav

Names that also work: bd, sd, cp, rs, lt, ch / hh, oh, sh / perc. A name can have
extra text around it (BD_kick 01.wav). Any lane without a file uses the Classic kit.
16, 24 or 32-bit WAV, mono or stereo, any sample rate. Up to 2.5 seconds is used.
Levels are matched to the Classic kit, so you don't need to normalize anything.

Restart Bricklab after adding a kit. Pick it on the drum track: SONG > GRID with
track 1 selected, then Y+X, or the Sound row of track 1's effects page.
