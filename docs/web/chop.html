SETTINGS · THE HANDHELD'S OWN PAGES · CLICK A VALUE TO STEP IT, OR USE ‹ ›

Pads: 1234 / QWER / ASDF (hold Shift to play softer; on a touch screen, press harder). Groups: Z drums, X bass, C keys, V your samples. Space plays, Enter records what you play into the group's loop, on the 16ths; hold Enter and hit a pad to erase it. K puts the last pad's sound across all 12 pads. The left stick tunes the last pad (up and down) and sets its level (sideways); the right stick is punch-in FX while you hold it. MIDI pads from C2 play pads 1 to 12, with velocity.
