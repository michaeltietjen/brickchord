press a chord

1–7 or A S D F G H J play the seven chords of the key. Hold Q for a 7th, W sus4, E 9th, R to flip major and minor. Space starts and stops the beat. Z and X step through the play modes: Chord, Strum, Slop, Harp, Arp and Repeat (the patterns follow the BPM). A MIDI keyboard works too in Chrome and Edge: each key plays the chord on that degree.

The status line shows the delay between pressing a key and hearing it. Lower is tighter; if you hear clicks, pick Safe.
