SETTINGS · THE HANDHELD'S OWN PAGES · CLICK A VALUE TO STEP IT, OR USE ‹ ›

1–7 or A–J play the seven chords of the key, and every voice glides there. The same chord again lets it fade. Hold Q for a 7th, W sus4, both for a 9th, E flips major and minor. Tap Shift to bloom (every voice from one note); hold it with a chord for a Move. Space plays the progression; V switches the screen between the glide guides and a spectrogram; while it plays the keys are melody notes over it, and the left stick strums (sideways) or arpeggiates (up and down). A MIDI keyboard or chord controller plays chords too.
